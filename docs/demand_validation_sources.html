Heat / Cool Demand — Final Sources

companion to notebooks/heat_demand_sandbox.ipynb & notebooks/cooling_demand_sandbox.ipynb — rewritten 2026-08-08
This page documents only what the two currently-deployed formulas are actually built on and checked against — no rejected or superseded material. Both notebooks went through many tried-and-abandoned formulas over the course of this work (Eurostat-only, US-EIA-only, various pooled compromises, a China provincial dataset that gave a real but too-weak signal, a declined bulk AC-electricity dataset, and more). None of that is repeated here. The full exploration history, including everything that was tried and rejected and why, lives in docs/demand_work_summary_2026-07-31.md — read that if you want the "why not X" reasoning; read this page if you just want to know what the final numbers are actually built on.

Methodology notes

HDD/CDD basis. All degree-day figures use the same convention as the rest of this pipeline: HDD base 18°C, CDD base 24°C, annual totals, matching climate_extractor.py's ERA5-Land methodology.

Why the fitting panels don't overlap with the independent checks. The heat formula's 426-point fitting panel is country/prefecture/state-level (Eurostat, Japan, US, Chile); the 15-city check is city-level and deliberately not part of that fit, so it's a genuine independent check rather than a re-statement of the training data. The cool formula's 13-point panel is city-level and is the fitting data — there is currently no separate independent check for cooling beyond the fit itself.
